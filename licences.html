Voices

The speech in PolyFox Spanish was generated in advance with open neural voices from the Piper project. They are synthetic voices, not recordings of people reading these sentences. The speech software itself is not part of the app.

  • "davefx", a voice from Spain, released into the public domain (CC0).
  • "sharvard", made from the Sharvard corpus of Spanish sentences (Edinburgh DataShare), under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0.
