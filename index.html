How it works

A little every day beats a lot now and then. PolyFox Spanish is built around a few minutes a day.

  1. Set your goal

    Choose a daily goal. If you already know some Spanish, the level check finds your starting unit.

  2. Do a lesson

    About four minutes: a few new words with pictures and sound, then sentences to build, hear, type and say.

  3. Let it come back

    What you learned returns in later lessons and in practice, more often when you find it hard and less when you know it.

Everything is in, and all of it is free

Nothing below is held back for a paid tier, because there is no paid tier.

The course

A1 to C2
180 units and 900 lessons: 2,160 words, about 2,880 sentences and 540 grammar points, all with spoken audio in voices from Spain.
The whole framework
All six levels of the European framework, from your first hola to the precision of C2.
Start where you are
A level check places you on the path if you already know some Spanish.
Your own form
Tell it whether to teach "estoy cansado" or "estoy cansada". Both are always accepted.

Lessons

Fifteen kinds of exercise
Pictures, matching pairs, word tiles, typing, listening, fill the gap, choose the form, spot the error, dialogues, reading and speaking.
Every correct answer counts
Tú, usted, vosotros or ustedes, with or without yo, numbers as digits: about 800 sentences accept other correct wordings, not just one.
Mistakes explained
A wrong answer shows which words were wrong and why: gender, ser and estar, negatives, agreement, verb endings, ñ and accents.
Tap any word
Every sentence can be tapped word by word for the meaning it has in that sentence, and verbs open their full table.

Speaking and listening

Speaking checked on your phone
Say the sentence and a speech model inside the app shows which words were understood. Nothing is sent anywhere.
Conversations
Twelve conversations set in Spain, from breakfast in a Madrid bar to a job interview in Málaga. Say or type your part, and the other person answers.
Dictation
Hear whole sentences and write them down.
Slow playback
Every sentence can be played at normal speed or slowly.

Reading and reference

Forty-two stories
Short stories from unit 8 to C1, read aloud one line at a time, with three questions at the end.
Verb tables
439 verbs in twelve tenses, with the gerund, the participle and every command form.
A grammar book
All 370 rules from the course in fifteen chapters, searchable.
An exam for each level
Thirty questions drawn from the whole level, with no hints. Score 70% to open the next level.

Keeping going

Smart review
Reviews are timed by FSRS, the method behind Anki, so each word comes back just before you would forget it.
A daily goal you choose
Five to twenty minutes, a streak, and one optional reminder at a time you set.
No lives to lose
Mistakes simply come back until you have them right. A five-lives challenge mode is there if you want it.
Up to five people
Each person on the phone has their own progress, streak and settings.

For everyone

Screen readers
Buttons, switches and answers are labelled for TalkBack, and results are read out.
Large text
Screens adapt to the text size set on your phone.
Light and dark
Two themes, and a reduce-motion switch.
A backup you hold
Save everyone's progress to a file and restore it on another phone.

Your learning is your business

  • No internet at all

    PolyFox Spanish has no permission to go online. It cannot send your progress, your voice or anything else anywhere.

  • Nothing to sign up for

    No account, no adverts and no tracking, in the app or on this site.

  • The microphone is yours

    It is used only when you tap record. Your words are recognised by a speech model inside the app, and the recording never leaves the phone.

Installing and updating

PolyFox Spanish is not on Google Play, so your phone asks a couple of extra questions the first time.

  1. Download the app file

    Tap Download on your phone. The file is called PolyFox.apk and is about 149 MB, so wifi is best. If Chrome says the file might be harmful, choose Download anyway.

  2. Allow the install

    Open the file. The first time, Android asks you to allow installs from your browser. Allow it, go back, and tap Install.

  3. Open PolyFox Spanish

    Type a name, choose a daily goal, and start the first lesson.

To update, download the newest version and install it over the old one. Your progress, streak and settings are kept.

Questions

Is PolyFox Spanish really free?

Yes. There is no paid version, no subscription, no trial and no adverts, and nothing in the app is locked behind payment.

Is it on Google Play?

No. PolyFox Spanish is a personal project and is downloaded from this site as an Android app file. It needs Android 8 or later.

Does it need the internet?

No. The app does not ask Android for internet access at all, so it works anywhere and cannot go online. Check for updates and More apps only open a page in your browser.

How does it check my speaking without the internet?

The app carries its own speech model (Whisper Base, running through whisper.cpp). It turns your recording into words on the phone and compares them with the sentence. It is lenient about words that sound alike but strict about meaning, so a wrong number or a missing negative does not pass. You can always mark yourself instead.

Why is the download so large?

Every word and sentence has its own recording, over 7,700 clips, and the speech model is about 80 MB. Nothing is downloaded later.

Is it the Spanish of Spain?

Yes. The course teaches the Spanish spoken in Spain, with vosotros and words such as móvil, ordenador and zumo, and its conversations and stories are set in Spain. Short notes show what you would hear in Latin America instead, and the speaking check accepts either accent.

Do I have to type the accents?

The ñ counts from the first lesson, because it changes the word. Other accents count from level B1; before that a missing accent is pointed out but your answer still passes. ¿ and ¡ never count against you.

Are there other languages?

Each language has its own app, so every course can be made as good as this one. PolyFox French is out too, and installs beside this one.

How far does the course go?

All the way: A1, A2, B1, B2, C1 and C2, from hola to irony, rhetoric, every tense of the subjunctive and the language of law, science and the arts.

Are the voices real people?

They are synthetic voices, made in advance and stored in the app. They are credited on the licences page.

Who wrote the course?

The course is written for PolyFox and has not yet been reviewed by native speakers. If something looks wrong, tap the flag after a question, or "My answer is right" after an answer, and share the list from Settings.

How do I update?

Download the newest version from this site and install it over the one you have. Your progress is kept. The app cannot check for updates itself because it cannot go online.
