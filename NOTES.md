The first version of PolyFox Spanish: the Spanish of Spain, from the first word to C2. It installs beside PolyFox French, not over it.

- 180 units from A1 to C2, written for Spain: vosotros from the start, Spanish words such as móvil and zumo, and short notes on what you would hear in Latin America.
- Every word and sentence spoken in voices from Spain, over 7,700 clips.
- Speaking checked on the phone, offline. Spanish and Latin American accents are both accepted; a wrong number or a missing no does not pass.
- Every correct answer counts: tú, usted, vosotros or ustedes, with or without yo, and the man's or woman's form you chose.
- The ñ counts from the first lesson; other accents count from B1. ¿ and ¡ never count against you.
- Twelve conversations set in Spain, from breakfast in a Madrid bar and the queue at a Valencia market to a complaint with the hoja de reclamaciones.
- Forty-two stories, six of them in A1, read aloud with three questions each.
- A verb book of 439 verbs in twelve tenses and a grammar book of 370 rules in fifteen chapters.
- Mistakes explained: gender, ser and estar, agreement, verb endings, negatives, ñ and accents.
- Reviews timed by FSRS, an exam for each level, up to five people on one phone, and a backup file you keep.
